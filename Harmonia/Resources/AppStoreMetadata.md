## 5. App Description

```
Master visual composition before you press the shutter.

Harmonia transforms your iPhone into a live photography and art coach. Inspired by centuries of classical art theory, Harmonia overlays 15 mathematical and artistic composition guides directly onto your camera viewfinder in real time.

Whether you are capturing portraits, architecture, landscapes, or street photography, Harmonia trains your eye to balance subjects and compose unforgettable photos.

ENGINEERED FOR IPHONE 15
• Triple Focal Length Precision: Instant, seamless switching between 0.5x (13mm Ultra-Wide), 1x (26mm Main), and 2x (52mm Lossless Sensor Crop).
• 48-Megapixel Master Capture: Capture pristine, full-resolution photos directly from the iPhone 15's high-resolution sensor into Apple Photos.
• Kinetic Horizon Level: 60 Hz hardware gyroscope tracking with a physical Taptic Engine snap when your camera reaches 0.0° level.

THE 15 COMPOSITION FRAMEWORKS
• Standard Grids: Rule of Thirds, Phi Grid, Symmetry.
• Classical & Fine Art: Harmonic Armature (14-line Renaissance grid), Golden Triangles, Dynamic Symmetry.
• Perspective & Geometry: Fibonacci Spiral, Vanishing Point, Leading Lines.
• Visual Flow & Spatial Depth: Framing Depth, Landscape Depth (3-Layer), Fill the Frame, Negative Space, Lines & Patterns, Left to Right.

SMART COACH (ON-DEVICE VISION AI)
• Unsure which rule to use? Harmonia's Smart Coach runs locally on Apple's Neural Engine to analyze your scene in real time.
• Detects portraits, group scenes, converging street lines, and landscapes to recommend the optimal composition rule, focal length, and camera angle.
• Tap once to apply the recommendation instantly, or shoot freely in full manual control.

DUAL-SAVE ASSET WORKFLOW
• Save Clean Master: Pristine, uncompressed 48MP/24MP photos saved straight to your Photos library.
• Save Composition Reference: Optionally saves an annotated reference photo with guide lines and telemetry baked in—ideal for art students, painters, and portfolio critique.

100% PRIVATE & ON-DEVICE
• No accounts. No tracking. No cloud uploads.
• All Vision analysis and photo processing runs 100% locally on your iPhone.
```
